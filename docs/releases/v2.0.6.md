# v2.0.6: 修复切换耳机后启动听写时应用闪退

修复一个让应用"无声消失"的问题：刚连上或断开耳机后开始听写，应用可能直接退出，没有任何提示。

## 🐛 Bug 修复

- 修复了输入设备刚变化（如 AirPods 连接/断开、插拔耳机）后按下听写快捷键时，应用直接闪退且无任何提示的问题。现在同样的情况只会提示"麦克风启动失败"，重新按一次快捷键即可继续使用。

# v2.0.6: Fix App Vanishing When Starting Dictation After a Headset Switch

Fixes an issue where the app could "silently disappear": starting dictation right after connecting or disconnecting a headset could quit the app with no message at all.

## 🐛 Bug fixes

- Fixed the app quitting instantly with no warning when the dictation hotkey was pressed right after the input device changed (AirPods connecting/disconnecting, plugging or unplugging a headset). The same situation now shows a "Starting the microphone failed" message instead — press the hotkey again and dictation continues normally.
