# v2.0.2: 启动更稳、不再卡死、退出崩溃修复

修复了几位用户遇到的「开始听写卡住不动」的问题，让每次按下快捷键都有确定的结果。

## 🐛 Bug 修复

- 修复了偶发的听写启动卡死：此前在电脑休眠唤醒后第一次听写，胶囊可能一直停在「准备中」，界面甚至整体无响应。现在启动过程全程不阻塞界面；如果 15 秒内仍未能开始录音，会明确提示「启动超时」并自动复位，重按快捷键即可重试。
- 修复了在上述卡死后点击菜单栏「退出」时应用崩溃的问题，现在退出始终干净利落。
- 网络不佳时，热词同步不再拖慢录音连接的建立（等待时间从最长约 1 分钟缩短到 10 秒内）。

# v2.0.2: More Reliable Startup, No More Freezes, Quit Crash Fixed

Fixes the "dictation gets stuck when starting" issue some users hit, so every hotkey press now has a definite outcome.

## 🐛 Bug fixes

- Fixed occasional freezes when starting dictation: previously the first attempt after waking your Mac from sleep could leave the capsule stuck at "Preparing" — sometimes freezing the whole app. Startup no longer blocks the interface; if recording cannot begin within 15 seconds, a clear "Starting dictation timed out" message appears and the app resets itself so you can simply try again.
- Fixed a crash when choosing Quit from the menu bar after the freeze described above — quitting is now always clean.
- On a poor network, hot-word syncing no longer delays the recording connection (worst-case wait cut from about a minute to under 10 seconds).
